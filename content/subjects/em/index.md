# Engineering Mathematics

Linear algebra, calculus, and probability & statistics. About 13 marks of every paper come from Engineering Mathematics (with Discrete Mathematics); these three areas alone have given 6–9 marks in each recent paper, and they are the most predictable marks in GATE.

**A**: master it and solve all its 2007–2023 questions. **B**: learn the method, then solve its questions. **C**: read once and keep its recall cards; solve its questions only if time is left. Keep the 2024–2026 papers unseen: they are your full-length mocks.

**How to study it**

- Probability and linear algebra are 63 of the 78 marks in the last 10 papers; calculus adds about 1.5 marks a paper.
- The questions are short calculations. Work through the 2007–2023 questions topic by topic with a timer (2 minutes for a 1-mark question, 4 for a 2-mark one).
- Memorise only the distribution table (mean and variance) and the eigenvalue shortcuts; everything else is derivable.

## Syllabus (GATE 2027)

> **Linear Algebra:** Matrices, determinants, system of linear equations, eigenvalues and eigenvectors, LU decomposition.
>
> **Calculus:** Limits, continuity and differentiability, Maxima and minima, Mean value theorem, Integration.
>
> **Probability and Statistics:** Random variables, Uniform, normal, exponential, Poisson and binomial distributions. Mean, median, mode and standard deviation. Conditional probability and Bayes theorem.

Unchanged from 2026. Numerical methods are no longer in the syllabus.

## How to prepare

1. **Probability first:** conditional probability and Bayes, expectation and variance, and the five named distributions. Expect at least one NAT.
2. **Linear algebra:** eigenvalue shortcuts (trace, determinant, powers), rank and consistency of systems, LU factorisation by hand.
3. **Calculus:** limits with L'Hôpital, maxima/minima on closed intervals, definite integrals using symmetry; one question per paper on average.
4. Practise on the **virtual calculator**: these are the questions where arithmetic speed decides the time you have left.
