## How to use this site

1. Read the [syllabus](#/syllabus) and [exam pattern](#/exam-pattern) once, so you know the rules of the game.
2. Follow the [strategy](#/strategy): high-weightage subjects first, then the rest, then mocks.
3. For each subject: skim the overview, study the notes, then drill its previous-year questions. Keep the formula sheet for daily revision.
4. In the last eight weeks, take the [mock test](mock/) under exam conditions and log every mistake.

> [!TIP] Search everything
> Press <kbd>/</kbd> anywhere to search all notes and formula sheets, for example "TLB", "Bayes" or "LL(1)".
