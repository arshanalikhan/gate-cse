# Past papers & answer keys

Every GATE CSE paper used for the [topic analysis](#/analysis), with the official answer key where one exists. Click a PDF to open it; all files are hosted on this site so they keep working after the official pages change.

> [!NOTE] How the papers were verified
> - **2024, 2025, 2026**: byte-for-byte identical to the PDFs on the official GATE 2027 site (SHA-256 match).
> - **2021, 2022, 2023**: compared with the official PDFs and answer keys from the GATE 2027 site; the text is the same paper.
> - **2007–2020**: no official copies are online any more. Each was checked for the right year, organising institute, paper code and question count, and every question was read during the analysis.

## How to use these papers

1. **Solve 2021–2026 under exam conditions.** These ten papers use today's pattern (MCQ, MSQ and NAT). Give yourself 3 hours, use only an on-screen scientific calculator (GATE gives you a virtual one, not a physical calculator), and score yourself with the official key and the GATE negative marking.
2. **Solve the IIT Madras paper of 2019 as a mock.** IIT Madras sets GATE 2027; its last paper shows the examiners' taste for C tracing and OS synchronisation.
3. **Use older papers by topic, not by year.** 2007–2020 papers are best for practising one topic at a time. Each subject's **Previous-year questions** tab lists every question by topic with a link to the right PDF.
4. **Keep an error log.** For every question you get wrong, write down the concept you missed and redo the question a week later.

> [!TIP] Answer keys for older papers
> Official keys for 2007–2014, 2016 and 2018 are no longer published. Where a PDF has no key, work the answer out from the subject's Notes and Formulae pages, and compare with a peer before trusting it.
